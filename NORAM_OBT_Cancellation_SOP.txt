# OBT Cancellation and Self-Booking Support SOP
SAMPLE DOCUMENT - fictional, for demonstration. Explains how agents support travellers who book in the online booking tool (OBT), such as Concur or Cytric.

# OBT refund of a self-booked ticket
1. Ask the traveller to cancel the trip in the OBT first; the OBT sends the booking to the offline queue.
2. Open the PNR from the OBT queue and check the ticket status is OPEN.
3. Check the fare rules (Category 16 and 33) for the refund conditions.
4. Process the refund in the GDS and add the remark RM*OBT REFUND PROCESSED in the PNR.
5. Update the OBT trip status to Cancelled and email the traveller the refund amount.
Important: never cancel a self-booked trip only in the GDS - the OBT will not show the change.

# Traveller cannot log in to the OBT
1. Check the traveller profile is active in the OBT admin screen.
2. Reset the password from the admin screen and ask the traveller to use the single sign-on link.
3. If single sign-on fails, raise a ticket with the OBT support team with a screenshot of the error.

# OBT booking not ticketed
1. Check the OBT queue for bookings waiting for approval.
2. If approval is pending, remind the approver; ticketing happens automatically after approval.
3. If approval is done but there is no ticket after 2 hours, issue the ticket offline and add the remark RM*OBT OFFLINE TKT.
